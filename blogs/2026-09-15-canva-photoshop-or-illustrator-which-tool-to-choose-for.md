A practical guide to understanding the differences between Canva, Photoshop, and Illustrator and knowing when to use each tool based on your creative goals. Many people start a visual project by wondering which tool they should use: Canva, Photoshop, or Illustrator. The answer almost always depends on the result you want to achieve, not on which of the three is more prestigious or seems more “professional.” Choosing the right tool from the start saves time, improves the quality of your work, and helps you avoid problems later on when exporting, printing, resizing, or editing a design.
