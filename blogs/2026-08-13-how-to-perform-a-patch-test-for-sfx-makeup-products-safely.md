Learn how to test liquid latex, skin-safe adhesive, and scar wax before applying special effects makeup to your face or body. Creating realistic wounds, scars, and prosthetic effects can transform a makeup look, but safety should always come before creativity. Products used in special effects makeup may contain ingredients that irritate the skin or trigger an allergic reaction, even when they are labeled as suitable for cosmetic use.
