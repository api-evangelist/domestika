A practical guide to identifying common flaws in your creative portfolio and improving how you present your work, your process, and your professional profile. In many creative processes, your portfolio is the first impression a client, agency, school, or company gets of you. Even before an interview or meeting takes place, that selection of projects is already communicating how you work, what kinds of problems you solve, your skill level, and what you can truly contribute.
