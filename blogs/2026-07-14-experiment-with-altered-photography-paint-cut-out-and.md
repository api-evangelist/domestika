A photograph can also be a starting point When we take a photograph, we tend to think that the process ends when we press the shutter button or, at most, after a little digital editing. However, an image can also be the beginning of a new creation. All it takes is printing it out and viewing it as a blank canvas to discover that it still has much to say.
