A selection of simple ideas to spark your imagination, fill your sketchbook, and get back to drawing when you’re facing a blank page. Creative block doesn’t always stem from a lack of talent. It often arises from being too hard on yourself, accumulated fatigue, constantly comparing your work to others’, or simply the fear of ruining a blank page.
