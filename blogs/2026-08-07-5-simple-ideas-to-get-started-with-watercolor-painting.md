Discover 5 easy exercises to help you overcome your fear of water, practice mixing colors, and create your first watercolor paintings step by step. The main idea behind this article is simple: starting with short, accessible exercises helps you understand how colors react when combined, how glazes are created, and how to make the most of those effects that arise unexpectedly. You don’t need to master the technique from day one; just observe, repeat, and enjoy the process.
