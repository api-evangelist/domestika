Discover how textile activities can help you slow down, focus on the present, and create a space of calm through your hands. There’s a moment—almost always unexpected—when a project starts to flow on its own. The needle goes in and out, the thread glides along, the yarn takes shape between your fingers, and, without realizing it, your thoughts have slowed down.
