From editorial illustration to digital collage and three-dimensional compositions: discover the main areas of illustration and the visual trends shaping the year. Illustration is present in far more places than we usually imagine. It can accompany a newspaper article, bring a children’s book to life, explain a scientific process, define a brand’s visual identity, or serve as the starting point for a video game.
