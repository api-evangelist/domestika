Learn how to develop an initial idea, give it structure, and transform it into a creative piece, series, or project with purpose, coherence, and potential for growth. Most creative projects don’t stem from a complete vision, but rather from a small observation: a phrase overheard in passing, a shape that catches your eye, an emotion that’s hard to put into words, an everyday object, a photograph saved for no apparent reason, or simply a question that keeps circling in your mind. The central idea of this article is simple: a small intuition doesn’t have to remain a loose sketch in a notebook.
