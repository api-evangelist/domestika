Discover how Fontalog expands typeface search beyond just the name or category, allowing you to explore fonts by style, proportion, contrast, weight, and optical qualities. Often, designers aren’t looking for a specific font, but rather a certain feel: something more condensed, more geometric, heavier, or with greater contrast than what they already have in front of them. Conventional typeface search engines are rarely designed to address this kind of uncertainty, because they’re still organized around broad categories or names that you need to know in advance.
