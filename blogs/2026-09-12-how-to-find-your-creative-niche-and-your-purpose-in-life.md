A guide to identifying what drives you, what you’re good at, who you can add value to, and how to turn your creative interests into a meaningful direction. You don’t need to have everything figured out to get started; many creative people feel pressure to find “their big purpose” or “their perfect niche” before taking any action. However, clarity usually comes the other way around: after exploring, creating, sharing, and paying close attention to what resonates with you and others.
