Discover why exploring new techniques, influences, and forms of expression can help you unlock your creativity and grow without losing your identity Finding your own style is often seen as one of the main goals for any creative person. It’s easy to think that the sooner you develop a recognizable aesthetic, the more cohesive your work will be and the easier it will be to build an artistic identity. However, that same idea can also become a source of pressure.
