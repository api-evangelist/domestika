Learn how to choose the right typeface based on the purpose, tone, format, and readability requirements of each visual project. Typography is not a minor detail in a design project. Even before reading a single word, a typeface already conveys a certain feeling: elegance, approachability, tradition, modernity, energy, authority, playfulness, luxury, calm, or technology.
