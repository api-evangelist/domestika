Discover simple practices to rekindle your curiosity, identify the topics that inspire you, and develop a more personal and mindful approach to creating There are times when creating comes easily: ideas flow naturally, time flies by without you noticing, and each project opens the door to the next. But there are also times when everything seems harder. You stare at a blank page without knowing where to start; you feel like you’re repeating the same solutions, or that whatever you do looks too much like someone else’s work.
