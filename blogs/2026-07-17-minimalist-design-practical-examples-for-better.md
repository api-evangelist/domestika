Discover how to reduce visual noise, prioritize information, and make clearer design decisions without resorting to cold or generic compositions. There’s a widespread misconception about minimalist design: that it consists of using white backgrounds, two typefaces, and a neutral color palette. However, you only need to look at some of the most renowned projects in contemporary design to see that this isn’t always the case.
