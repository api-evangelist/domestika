Simple decorating, organizing, and personalization ideas to give your home a fresh look with your own two hands—without major renovations or a big budget. Giving a room a fresh look can start with much smaller steps than you might think: painting a wall, reorganizing a bookshelf, creating a decorative piece, changing the textiles, or giving a piece of furniture you already own a makeover. You don’t need major construction or a big budget to make a difference.
