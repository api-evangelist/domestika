Learn how to incorporate a brief, consistent, and flexible creative practice for drawing, writing, observing, or experimenting without pressure. Many people give up on their creative pursuits because they’re waiting for the perfect moment: a whole free afternoon, the right energy, or the inspiration needed to get started. The problem is that this ideal moment almost never comes, and waiting ends up pushing us further and further away from our creative practice.
