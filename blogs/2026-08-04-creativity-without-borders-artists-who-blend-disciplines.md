Discover how different contemporary artists combine illustration, photography, sculpture, fashion, design, animation, and craftsmanship to create unique creative worlds. For a long time, our education and the professional world have taught us to pigeonhole ourselves: illustrator, photographer, industrial designer, or sculptor. However, the most avant-garde ideas rarely respect the administrative boundaries of the fine arts.
